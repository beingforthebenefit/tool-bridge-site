Privacy

Tool Bridge collects nothing. There are no analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

  • Your servers, tools, recipes, settings and request history are stored on your devices and synced through your own private iCloud database.
  • Tokens and API keys stay in your Keychain.
  • Requests are handled by Apple's on-device model first. When it needs help, a request may go to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, or to an AI provider you added with your own key; the app's History shows exactly what was sent to which.
  • Requests to your MCP servers go straight from your device to those servers.
